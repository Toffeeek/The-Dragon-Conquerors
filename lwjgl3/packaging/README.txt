THE DRAGON CONQUERORS

Double-click The Dragon Conquerors.exe. Play.bat is an optional shortcut.
No separate Java, Gradle, IDE, or source checkout is required.
Keep the entire folder together, including app and runtime, when copying the game.
PRACTICE lets you choose any map alone; it is restricted to this computer.
HOST LAN starts a four-player, two-versus-two lobby with map voting.
Friends on the same network choose JOIN LAN and enter the lobby's invite address.
The host must stay running for the match to continue.

Windows may ask you to allow local-network access. Only allow networks you trust.
The bundled runtime's license notices are in runtime/legal; keep those notices.
Internet matchmaking and public-server deployment are not included.

Sound, training targets, and bots are not included.
